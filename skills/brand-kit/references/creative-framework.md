# Creative framework

The strategy that the visual system expresses. Written by you as a proposal, approved by the client; it fills
`system.json` → `platform`, `voice`, `messaging`, and the `mechanism` of each route. Tags: `[strong]` `[limited]`
`[practice]`.

## Order of work `[practice]`
1. **Listen**: brief, site, competitors, the client's own words. Quote them; don't paraphrase them into jargon.
2. **Platform** (one screen, no more): purpose → positioning → audience → personality → values → promise.
3. **Voice**: 3-4 traits as "is / is not" pairs.
4. **Territories**: 2-4 creative directions, each a different answer to "what is this brand like?"
   One territory = one route = one **mechanism** sentence.
5. Only then: marks, type, colour (`cohesion.md`).

## Platform fields
| Field | Write | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| `purpose` | Why it exists beyond money, one sentence, in the client's terms. | "To inspire the world" filler. |
| `positioning` | For [audience], [brand] is the [frame] that [difference], because [reason to believe]. | A difference every competitor can claim. |
| `audience` | Who decides and who uses; a situation, not demographics only. | "Everyone". |
| `archetype` | Optional, one (Jungian / Mark & Pearson): Sage, Explorer, Caregiver… as shorthand only. `[limited]` | Treating it as science. |
| `personality` | 3-5 adjectives that map to the axes. | Synonyms of "quality". |
| `values` | 3 behaviours the team can be held to. | Integrity, passion, excellence. |
| `promise` | What a customer can count on every time. | A claim you cannot prove. |
| `story` | 3-5 sentences: origin, tension, what changes for the customer. | Invented founders, dates or numbers. |

Never invent facts (founding year, awards, numbers, client names). If unknown, write the structure and leave the
fact out, or ask.

## Voice and messaging
- `voice.summary`: one sentence on how the brand sounds.
- `voice.traits`: `[{"is": "Direct", "is_not": "Blunt"}, …]` 3-4 pairs; each pair names a risk the trait carries.
- `voice.tone_by_channel`: `[{"channel": "Instagram", "tone": "…"}, {"channel": "Support email", …}]`.
- `voice.vocabulary`: words to use (from the trade, the customers) and to avoid (clichés of the sector).
- Casing, I/you/we, emoji yes/no, exclamation marks: state them in `summary` or traits.
- `voice.sample_lines`: 4-6 real lines in the brand's language (headline, CTA, error message, caption).
- `voice.taglines`: 3-5 routes if the client has none; label them as proposals.
- `messaging.pillars`: 3 `{name, message, proof}`; `proof` is something the client can show, else leave empty.
- `messaging.elevator_pitch`: 2-3 sentences.

## Territories and mechanism
Each route answers the brief differently and stands apart on ≥ 2 axes (`cohesion.md`). Write per route:
- **Territory name** (2-3 words) and a one-line metaphor from the brand's real world (a tool, a material, a
  place, a gesture, a craft) — not from the design world.
- **Mechanism**: "one idea, three expressions" ("a ledger's ruled line: hairline-cut wordmark, rule-thin symbol,
  ink-on-paper palette").
- **Expression move**: the only loud thing (mark, type or colour).
- **Graphic devices** it yields: a pattern from the mark, a crop, a rule, a frame, a corner, a highlight shape.
  These go to `system.json → graphic.devices` `[{"name", "description"}]` for the chosen route.
- **Where it differs** from the named competitors (`differs_by`).

## Mood and references
Describe a mood in words (materials, light, era, places) rather than collecting images; when citing precedent,
link live sources (`kit.md` → Inspiration), never copy. A route that needs a reference image to be understood is
not yet an idea.

## Presenting
Six to ten lines per route at the checkpoint. Strategy first, then visuals. Recommend one route and say why from
the brief. Approvals are the client's; do not write "approved" for anything they have not chosen.
